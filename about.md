### Welcome to my blog!

Hello, I’m Talia! I started this blog as a place to share my love of birdwatching, and motivate me to continue taking the time to enjoy this hobby in and around Edinburgh, UK. On my page you’ll find a mixture of observational nature-writing and short scientific articles, which I hope you enjoy reading as much as I enjoy writing.

One of my goals for this blog is to eventually take as many of the photos as I can myself. I’m not a photographer by any means, but I’m absolutely in awe of wildlife photography, so I’m excited give it a go.

Follow along for birdwatching adventures!
